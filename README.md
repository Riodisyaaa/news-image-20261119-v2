# The World in One Day — Thursday 19 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026: 36 stories across 7 regions — 13 illustrated (3 heroes + 10 panels) and 23 briefs — with a world map, a numbers strip, and a date-check box listing items first reported on 18 November so they are not presented as 19 November news.

Built for make.fun's [news-image-20261119](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119) bounty ($250). The image is drawn by `draw_v2.py` (Python + PIL, no network) and copied to the output by `main.ts`.
